Privacy notice

This prototype was deliberately built to test the experience without collecting personal information.

What is not collected

  • No account, name, email address, birth date, birth time or location is requested.
  • No feedback is submitted to a server; the summary is generated inside your browser for you to copy.
  • No cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, social trackers or browser storage are used.

What happens in your browser

Your choices, ratings and notes exist only in the memory of the open page. Closing or reloading clears them. The invitation code is checked locally against a cryptographic hash.

Hosting

The static files are hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary connection information under its own terms. This prototype includes no WOW Development analytics or tracking code.

Purpose and limits

This is a limited user-experience prototype for invited adult testers. It is not a complete birth-chart service, not Sol Vartan's personal interpretation, not professional advice and not a launched commercial product.

Feedback and opt-out

Share feedback by copying the locally generated summary and replying through the original invitation channel. You can decline or request no further contact at any time.
